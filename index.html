Hi there! I'm Toby Lovick

Astrophysics PhD student at the University of Cambridge

My research focuses on scalable Bayesian inference for large-scale cosmological surveys. Applications include Type Ia supernovae, strong gravitational lensing, and gravitational wave noise marginalisation.
